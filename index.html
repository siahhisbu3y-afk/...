Our Mission

A server built on trust, not tolerance.

Most Discord servers react to problems after they escalate. CaseWatch was designed from the ground up to catch issues early, handle them properly, and protect everyone involved — members and staff alike.

We don't believe in sweeping things under the rug. Every report is taken seriously, every case is documented, and every decision is made with care.

🛡

Protecting Members

A confidential place to report harassment, scams, suspicious behavior, or unsafe situations — without fear of retaliation.

🔎

Real Investigations

Reports aren't just read and forgotten. Staff review evidence, organize cases, and follow up with actual outcomes.

⚖️

Fair Moderation

Actions are proportionate, logged, and reviewable. Members can appeal. Nothing is arbitrary.

📂

Transparent Records

Every case has a number, status, and paper trail. Staff accountability is built into the process.

"I didn't build this because I wanted power over a community. I built it because I was tired of watching people get hurt in spaces that were supposed to be safe."

I've been in enough Discord servers to know how badly things can go wrong. Staff that ignore reports. Moderators that abuse their position. Members who harass others and face zero consequences. I watched it happen over and over, and eventually I decided to stop waiting for someone else to build the right kind of server.

CaseWatch started as an idea — what if a community actually took safety seriously? Not just as a bullet point in the rules, but as the entire foundation? Every system here, from the ticket panel to the case numbers to the modlogs, was designed with one question in mind: if a member came to us scared or hurt, would they feel like we actually handled it?

I don't promise perfection. Staff are human, cases are complicated, and not every situation has a clean resolution. What I do promise is that we will always look, always document, and always give people a fair hearing. No one gets dismissed here. No report gets buried.

If you've been burned before by servers that didn't take you seriously — this one was made for you.

— s1ahhupn3xt, Founder
What We Do
01 —

Member Reports

Any member can open a private, confidential ticket to report rule-breaking, harassment, scams, or safety threats. Reports are reviewed by real staff, not bots.

02 —

Case Investigation

Serious reports become numbered cases. Staff assign investigators, add evidence and notes, track status, and update the involved parties — start to finish.

03 —

Structured Moderation

Warnings, mutes, bans, kicks, and timeouts are all logged with reasons and timestamps. Every action is tied to a staff member and reviewable.

04 —

Alert System

Staff can flag known bad actors on an alert board so the whole team stays informed when flagged users enter or interact in the server.

05 —

Appeals Process

Members who believe a punishment was unjust can submit a formal appeal. It goes directly to staff for review — no runarounds, no dismissals.

06 —

Staff Accountability

Modlogs are permanent and searchable. No staff member can act without it being recorded. Abuse of power is just as much a violation as anything else.
