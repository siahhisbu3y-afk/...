The Process

How we operate.

Every case goes through the same rigorous process — from the first report to the final outcome. Nothing is rushed, nothing is skipped.

01

Report Received

A member opens a private ticket or submits a report. Staff acknowledge it within hours, not days.

02

Case Opened

A numbered case is created. An investigator is assigned. Evidence collection begins immediately.

03

Investigation

We gather screenshots, logs, account info, patterns, and any additional witnesses. Everything is timestamped and stored.

04

Report to Discord

A full evidence package is submitted to Discord Trust & Safety. We follow up and track the outcome.

05

Public Exposure

If Discord is slow or the threat is ongoing, evidence is posted publicly across our platforms with full context.

06

Case Closed

The case is logged, archived, and the outcome is recorded. If the target evades, the case stays open.

Server Rules

How we expect everyone to conduct themselves.

These rules exist to protect the integrity of our investigations and the safety of our members.

Rule 01 —

No false reports

Filing a false report to target someone you dislike is a serious offence. You will be permanently banned and potentially exposed yourself.

Rule 02 —

Keep evidence intact

Do not edit, crop, or alter screenshots before submitting. Tampered evidence gets the case dismissed and you removed.

Rule 03 —

Confidentiality

Active cases and ticket contents are confidential. Do not share case details outside of designated channels.

Rule 04 —

No vigilante action

Do not confront, harass, or attempt to expose targets yourself. Let staff handle it properly so cases hold up.

Rule 05 —

Respect all members

This is a safety community — not a place for beef, drama, or power trips. Staff and members are held to the same standard.

Rule 06 —

Staff decisions are final

If you disagree with a moderation decision, open an appeal — don't argue in public channels. The process exists for a reason.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my report kept confidential?

Yes. Everything you share in a ticket is private between you and the staff team. We do not share reporter identities publicly. If evidence is posted, it is redacted to protect the victim.

What happens after I submit a report?

A case is opened, an investigator is assigned, and we begin collecting evidence. We'll update you through your ticket. Most cases get an initial response within a few hours.

Do you actually report to Discord?

Yes — every confirmed case gets a formal report to Discord Trust & Safety with a full evidence package. We track outcomes and follow up.

What if Discord doesn't ban them?

If Discord is slow or doesn't act, we escalate — posting evidence publicly on our social media so other communities can protect themselves. For serious cases involving minors, we escalate to law enforcement.

Can I report someone from outside this server?

Yes. You don't have to be a member of a specific server to report someone. If you have evidence of predatory, harassing, or scam behavior on Discord, we'll look into it.

What if I was wrongly punished in another server?

We have an appeals process. Open a ticket, explain what happened, and staff will review it. We're not here to side with mods automatically — we look at the actual evidence.
